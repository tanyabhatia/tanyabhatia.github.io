Hi! I'm a MS student in Biomedical Data Science at Stanford University. I build machine learning systems for multimodal health and clinical data, with a focus on physiological signal processing, time-series modeling, and algorithm development for real-world health applications. My work spans wearable biosignals, clinical LLMs, AI evaluation, foundation models, and medical imaging. I'm especially passionate about early detection of health conditions using advanced AI/ML methods. Feel free to get in touch!

Sleep · Wearables

Sleep Feature Extraction & Classification

Wearables · Pregnancy

Heart Rate and Respiratory Rate Dynamics as Biomarkers of Pregnancy

Analyzed four years of Apple Watch HR and respiratory rate data using change point detection to identify structural breaks in both signals, delineating a multi-month period of co-elevation consistent with pregnancy-related physiological adaptations. Delivery timing was detectable from the HR signal alone as a sharp structural break, without any clinical labels.

Change Point DetectionApple WatchTime SeriesSnyder Lab
Foundation Models · Wearables

Leveraging Foundation Models to Predict Anxiety Using Wearables Data

Investigated whether embeddings from pretrained time-series foundation models (MOMENT, TSPulse) can predict state anxiety from Fitbit physiological signals, using lightweight classification heads and LoRA fine-tuning. Benchmarked foundation model approaches against CNN, Random Forest, and SVM baselines on anchored wearable windows with STAI-derived anxiety labels.

LoRAMOMENTFitbitLOPO CVSnyder Lab
PPG · Deep Learning

Scalogram-Based Stress Classification from PPG

Converting raw 1D PPG biosignals to 2D scalogram representations via continuous wavelet transform (CWT), then benchmarking CNN, Vision Transformer, and hybrid CNN-ViT architectures for stress classification. Evaluated with Grad-CAM to identify discriminative signal regions.
